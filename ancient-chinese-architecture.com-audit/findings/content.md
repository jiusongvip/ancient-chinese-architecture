# Content Quality Findings

## E-E-A-T Assessment

### Good: Demonstrated expertise in subject matter
The content demonstrates strong subject-matter expertise. Building profiles cover architectural history, structural engineering (dougong), materials science, and cultural context. Dynasty pages trace 3,000 years of evolution with specific technical terminology.

### Medium: No author attribution
No author bylines, bios, or credentials are visible on any page. For an educational site about architectural history, author expertise signals strengthen E-E-A-T.

### Medium: No cited sources or references
Content is presented without citations to academic sources. Architectural historians like Liang Sicheng are mentioned in content but not cited as formal sources.

### Good: Transparent about project nature
The footer identifies the site as "A digital museum exploring 3,000 years of Chinese architectural heritage" and the About page provides context.

## Thin Content

### High: Truncated meta descriptions
Three pages have meta descriptions cut off mid-sentence:
- /buildings/forbidden-city/ — ends with "Built under the Yongle Emperor of the Ming dynasty, " (trailing space, truncated)
- /elements/dougong/ — ends with "placed atop colum"
- /elements/roofs/ — ends with "symbolic mean"

The descriptions appear to be the full description field from the data model rather than a crafted 150-160 character meta description.

### Medium: Gallery page content depth
The gallery page needs verification of its actual content. If primarily image-based without descriptive text per image, it may be treated as thin by search engines.

### Low: Blog section has previews but no detail pages
Three blog article previews appear on the homepage and blog listing, but no corresponding detail pages exist in the build output.

## Content Structure

### Good: Clear heading hierarchy on detail pages
Building and dynasty detail pages use consistent H1 > H2 > sidebar card structure.

### Good: Long-form, substantive content
Building detail pages contain 3-4 substantial content sections (300-500 words per page). Dynasty pages contain 150-200 word descriptions with feature lists.

### Good: FAQ uses semantic details/summary
The FAQ section uses native HTML disclosure widgets, providing structured, indexable Q&A content.

## Readability

### Good: Clear, accessible prose
Content is written at an educated general-audience level. Technical terms are explained in context.

### Good: Scannable feature lists
Key facts are presented as bullet-style feature lists with checkmark icons, improving scannability.

## Keyword Coverage

### Good: Strong topical authority signals
The site covers the full keyword spectrum: dynasties by era, building types, architectural elements, regional distribution, and practical travel information.

### Medium: Missing long-tail comparison content
The compare page exists but comparison content between dynasties could be expanded into individual pages for stronger long-tail keyword coverage.
